## Overview
**Miraidon ex Box** is an **Lightning toolbox deck** that leverages some of the strongest Energy acceleration and damage modifiers ever printed.
Rather than relying on a single attacker, the deck rotates between multiple **Lightning attackers** depending on the matchup, tempo, and Prize map.

My decklist stays true to the classic Miraidon Box philosophy but differs from the showcased list by:
- Adding **Iron Thorns ex**, **Zekrom ex**, and **Latias ex**
- Cutting Zeraora-GX and Stormy Mountains
- Leaning harder into **disruption Stadiums** (Temple of Sinnoh)
- Playing a more **aggressive damage-oriented build**

The result is a deck that can **beat Item lock (Vileplume)**, pressure one-Prize decks, and overwhelm multi-Prize strategies with speed and burst damage.

---

## Core Strategy

### Miraidon ex Engine
- **Miraidon ex – Tandem Unit**
  - Searches **2 Basic Lightning Pokémon** from the deck
  - Turns Miraidon into a universal Pokémon search engine
- Finding Miraidon often means finding:
  - Iron Hands ex
  - Vikavolt V
  - Regieleki V
  - Zekrom ex
  - Tapu Koko Prism Star

This makes the deck extremely consistent despite playing many single-copy attackers.

---

## Energy Acceleration

Lightning decks live and die by Energy speed — this list has plenty:

- **Electric Generator**
  - High-roll acceleration to the Bench
- **Max Elixir**
  - Strong early-game pressure
- **Tapu Koko Prism Star**
  - Instant Energy spread from discard
- **Thunder Mountain Prism Star**
  - Reduces attack costs, enabling turn 1 or turn 2 attacks

Unlike Standard, Expanded allows stacking all of these at once, leading to explosive turns.

## Attacker Toolbox

### Key Attackers
- **Miraidon ex**
  - *Photon Blaster*: Efficient two-Prize trades
- **Iron Hands ex**
  - *Amp You Very Much*: Extra Prize pressure vs one-Prize decks
- **Vikavolt V**
  - *Paralyzing Bolt*: Item lock to counter combo-based lists
- **Iron Thorns ex**
  - Ability lock attacker to counter decks like Vileplume or any Control
  - Very strong when combined with **Electropower**
  - Lock only needs to last long enough to gain a decisive Prize lead
- **Zekrom ex**
  - Massive raw damage
  - Excellent finisher when boosted by Electropower
- **Regieleki VMAX**
  - Passive Lightning damage boost
  - Secondary bulky attacker
  - Prevents auto-loss to anti-Basic techs
- **Tapu Koko Prism Star**
  - Energy acceleration and late-game attacker

## Damage Modifiers

One of Miraidon’s biggest strengths in Expanded:

- **Electropower (x4)**
  - Turns 2HKOs into OHKOs
  - Allows small attackers to punch far above their weight
- **Regieleki VMAX**
  - Permanent Lightning damage boost
- **Prime Catcher**
  - Gust + combo enabler for game-ending turns

This makes the deck extremely threatening even against high-HP Pokémon VSTAR, ex, and Tag Teams.

## Stadium & Tech Choices

- **Temple of Sinnoh**
  - Shuts down Special Energy-heavy decks
  - Very strong vs Lugia, Regidrago, and combo decks
- **Field Blower**
  - Removes Tools and Stadiums (Path to the Peak, Big Charm, etc.)
- **Float Stone**
  - Ensures smooth pivoting

---

## Tips & Play Notes

- Do not overcommit attackers early — choose the **right attacker for the matchup**
- Iron Hands ex should be used surgically to steal Prize leads
- Vikavolt V doesn’t need to lock forever — one or two turns can win the game
- Electropower timing is critical; plan your turns ahead
- Temple of Sinnoh can win games on its own if dropped at the right moment
- Accept that the deck benches many liabilities — speed is your defense

---

## Strengths
- Extremely fast and explosive
- Flexible matchup coverage
- Strong vs Item lock decks like Vileplume
- High damage ceiling

## Weaknesses
- Relies on lucky acceleration
- Benches many two-Prize Pokémon
- Can struggle vs heavy Energy denial if Tapu Koko Prism Star is lost

## Summary
Miraidon ex Box in Expanded is a **high-tempo Lightning toolbox** that thrives on speed, pressure, and adaptability.
My version leans more aggressive and disruptive than the stock list, trading some utility for raw power and Stadium control.

It may not always Prize trade perfectly, but when it hits, it hits *hard* — and often before the opponent can stabilize.
