# Tsareena V

![Decklist](expanded-decks/assets/decklist/Tsareena.png)

*(Personalized version – my decklist is slightly different and more disruptive)*

## Overview
**Tsareena V** is an **aggressive Prize-race deck** that abuses one of the most extreme damage mechanics in the format:
**discarding your own Bench to deal massive damage**.

My decklist keeps the explosive aggro core of Tsareena but leans harder into:
- **Ability disruption** (Garbodor, Klefki, Flutter Mane)
- **Bench recursion loops**
- **Expanded-specific consistency engines**

Rather than racing blindly, this version aims to **control how the opponent is allowed to play**, while still threatening OHKOs every turn.

---

## Core Strategy

### Tsareena V Damage Engine
- Tsareena V deals damage by **discarding Benched Pokémon**
- With **Sky Field (x4)**:
  - Bench size increases to 8
  - Tsareena reaches **300–340 damage**, enough to OHKO virtually anything
- You rarely need a full Bench — even partial discards hit key numbers

The deck accepts that Tsareena V will often be KO’d and focuses on **efficient Prize trading**.

---

## Bench Filling & Recursion

Expanded makes refilling the Bench trivial:

- **Exeggcute**
  - Recovers itself from the discard
  - Pays discard costs for Ultra Ball and Quick Ball
- **Giratina**
  - Recursion from the discard
  - Places damage counters on the opponent’s Bench
- **Battle Compressor**
  - Dumps recursion targets immediately

After using support Pokémon for their Abilities, you can discard them safely and reuse them later.

### Reuse Engines
- **Dragonite-EX**
  - Recovers Pokémon directly to the Bench
- **Rescue Stretcher / Night Stretcher**
  - Sustain Bench density throughout the game
- **Eldegoss V**
  - Recovers key Supporters for tempo turns

---

## Energy Acceleration & Tempo

- **Raihan**
  - Powers up a fresh Tsareena V in one turn
- **Tapu Koko Prism Star**
  - Emergency Energy acceleration
- Low Energy count is offset by constant attacker turnover

This keeps the deck fast and resilient despite frequent knockouts.

---

## Disruption Package (Key Difference)

My list heavily attacks **Abilities**, which are everywhere in Expanded:

- **Garbodor (Garbotoxin)**
  - Global Ability lock with a Tool attached
- **Klefki**
  - Shuts off Basic Pokémon Abilities
- **Flutter Mane**
  - Answers Active-only Ability Pokémon like Wobbuffet
- **Faba**
  - Permanently removes Tools and Special Energy

This disruption helps compensate for Tsareena’s natural weakness to Ability-based counters.

## Support Pokémon & Consistency

- **Tapu Lele-GX**
  - Guaranteed Supporter access
- **Dedenne-GX / Crobat V / Squawkabilly ex**
  - Explosive draw power
- **Latias ex**
  - Mobility and board control
- **Forest Seal Stone**
  - One-time universal search
- **Prime Catcher**
  - Gust + combo enabler

These Pokémon are used aggressively, then discarded for Tsareena’s attack.

---

## Stadium Control

- **Sky Field (x4)**
  - Central to the deck’s damage output
  - Forces opponents to respect Bench size
- Losing Sky Field mid-turn can be dangerous — plan discards carefully

---

## Matchup Tools

- **Vs Ability-heavy decks**
  - Garbodor + Klefki can completely shut down setup
- **Vs Bench control (Sudowoodo)**
  - Giratina counters help soften targets
  - Prime Catcher enables targeted knockouts
- **Vs Control**
  - Constant OHKO pressure limits their setup time

---

## Tips & Play Notes

- Always count Bench slots before committing to an attack
- Treat support Pokémon as **temporary resources**
- Do not overprotect Tsareena — assume it will be KO’d
- Garbodor timing is crucial; lock when the opponent is committed
- Giratina counters are not incidental — plan future turns with them
- Sky Field management often decides games

---

## Strengths
- Massive damage ceiling
- Fast, aggressive pressure
- Strong Ability disruption
- Excellent Prize-trading potential

## Weaknesses
- Reliant on Stadium presence
- Bench-limiting effects are dangerous
- Requires careful sequencing and resource tracking

---

## Summary
Tsareena V in this configuration is an **aggro-control hybrid**:
it hits like a true aggressive deck while actively **denying the opponent’s setup** through Ability lock and disruption.

My version sacrifices some raw speed for **interaction and resilience**, making it better suited to hostile, Ability-heavy metagames — and extremely punishing when opponents misstep.
