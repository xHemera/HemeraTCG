# Mew Vmax

![Decklist](expanded-decks/assets/decklist/Mew.png)

*(Personalized version – my decklist differs from the featured guide)*

## Overview
**Mew VMAX Path** is a **disruptive Fusion Strike deck** built around one of the most powerful draw engines ever printed: **Genesect V**.
Instead of racing purely on damage or bench size, this version focuses on **Ability denial**, **hand disruption**, and **resource control**, forcing the opponent to play at Mew’s pace.

My decklist stays close to the classic Mew VMAX shell but is tuned to be more **oppressive and grindy**, using Path to the Peak as a central win condition rather than an afterthought.

---

## Core Engine

### Fusion Strike Draw Engine
- **Genesect V – Fusion Strike System**
  - Draws until you have cards equal to the number of Fusion Strike Pokémon in play
  - Enables explosive turns and fast recovery after disruption
- **Mew VMAX**
  - Copies **Techno Blast** for massive damage
  - Damage is amplified by **Power Tablet**
- **Mew V**
  - *Psychic Leap* is crucial in long games
  - Shuffles **Double Colorless Energy** back into the deck to avoid Energy denial

This engine allows the deck to play very few Energy while maintaining constant pressure.

---

## Game Plan

### Disruption First, Damage Second
Unlike all-in aggressive builds, this version prioritizes:
- **Shutting off opposing Abilities**
- **Breaking hand consistency**
- **Removing key resources**

Damage comes naturally once the opponent is locked out.

---

## Stadium & Lock Package

- **Path to the Peak (x4)**
  - Disables:
    - Dedenne-GX / Crobat V
    - Lugia VSTAR
    - Regidrago VSTAR
    - Many setup-dependent engines
- **Marshadow**
  - Removes your own Path when needed
  - Essential for sequencing big Genesect turns
- **Faba / Lost Vacuum**
  - Permanently removes problematic cards
  - Strong against Special Energy and Tools

Path is not just a tech — it is a **primary win condition**.

---

## Damage Modifiers

- **Power Tablet (x4)**
  - Core damage boost
  - Allows Mew VMAX to reach OHKO thresholds
- **Muscle Band (x2)**
  - Enables key numbers:
    - 280 damage with only two Power Tablets
  - Excellent vs Stage 2 and bulky Pokémon ex

This combination allows precise damage mapping rather than overcommitting resources.

---

## Support Pokémon & Techs

- **Oricorio**
  - Reduces damage taken by Fusion Strike Pokémon
  - Allows Mew VMAX to tank attacks like:
    - Dragon Ascent + Muscle Band from Regidrago VSTAR
- **Budew**
  - Early-game Item disruption
  - Excellent vs setup-heavy decks
- **Marshadow**
  - Non-Fusion Pokémon to break Path locks
  - Adds flexibility to sequencing

---

## Trainer Choices & Utility

- **Battle VIP Pass**
  - Explosive turn 1 setup
- **Cram-o-matic**
  - Item-based search that synergizes with excess cards
- **Recycle**
  - Recovers key Items like Power Tablet or Lost Vacuum
- **VS Seeker**
  - Reuse disruption Supporters
- **Computer Search**
  - Universal consistency tool
- **Forest Seal Stone**
  - Once-per-game access to any card

The deck is designed to **burn cards aggressively**, then refill using Genesect V.

---

## Matchup Notes

- **Vs Regidrago**
  - Energy denial + Path pressure
  - Oricorio improves survivability
- **Vs Lugia**
  - Path to the Peak is often game-ending
- **Vs Control**
  - Psychic Leap is critical to preserve Energy
  - Avoid leaving Double Colorless Energy in play unnecessarily
- **Vs Setup Decks**
  - Budew + Path can completely stop early development

---

## Tips & Play Notes

- Always plan your **Path turns** carefully — lock first, draw later
- Use Psychic Leap proactively, not reactively
- Do not overbench Fusion Pokémon unless necessary
- Power Tablet sequencing matters more than raw quantity
- Marshadow is a resource — don’t waste it early
- This deck wins by **denying options**, not by racing

---

## Strengths
- Extremely consistent draw engine
- Powerful Ability lock
- Strong into top meta decks
- Low Energy commitment

## Weaknesses
- Relies heavily on Stadium control
- Three-Prize attackers are always a risk
- Mis-sequencing Genesect draws can cost games

---

## Summary
Mew VMAX Path is a **refined, disruptive evolution** of the classic Fusion Strike deck.
My version leans harder into control elements, using Path to the Peak as a proactive weapon rather than a defensive tool.

It proves that even in a format full of wild combos, **tight sequencing, denial, and efficiency** are still enough to win games.
