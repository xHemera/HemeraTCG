# Vileplume Bunnelby

![Decklist](expanded-decks/assets/decklist/BunnelbyVileplume.png)

## Overview
This is an **Expanded-format Vileplume Control deck** built around **Item lock** and **resource denial**, with **Slaking ex** as a heavy-hitting attacker instead of the more common Dragapult ex.
The deck aims to **establish a near-unbreakable lock early** using Vileplume, then maintain it throughout the game while answering threats efficiently.

The core engine revolves around **Guzma & Hala**, **Bunnelby**, and **TM: Evolution**, allowing extremely fast setup of multiple Stage 2 Pokémon.
Once the lock is in place, the deck wins through board control, disruption, and selective knockouts.

---

## Core Strategy

### Early Game Setup
- **Turn 1 goal**: Resolve **Guzma & Hala**
  - Search targets:
    - **Artazon**
    - **TM: Evolution**
    - **Jet Energy** or **Capture Energy** (depending on Active)
- There are **8 outs** to Guzma & Hala (including **Tag Call** and **Call Bell**).

- Ideal board:
  - **Bunnelby Active**
  - **Oddish + Pidgey on the Bench**
- **Fan Rotom** via Artazon helps fetch:
  - Bunnelby
  - Pidgey
  - Slakoth

### Evolution Chain
- Attach **TM: Evolution** to Bunnelby
- Use **Evolution**:
  - Oddish → Gloom
  - Pidgey → Pidgeotto
- Thanks to **Bunnelby’s Omega Barrage**, TM: Evolution can be used **twice in one turn**
- Result:
  - **Vileplume** (Item lock)
  - **Pidgeot ex** (search engine)

Once this board is established, the deck shifts into full control mode.

---

## Lock & Resource Control

- **Vileplume** shuts down Items, crippling most Expanded decks
- **Pidgeot ex** ensures consistent access to key cards every turn
- **Grand Tree (Ace Spec)** allows continued access to Stage 2 Pokémon when needed
- **Lusamine** recovers:
  - Stadiums (Path to the Peak, Grand Tree)
  - Supporters
- **Bunnelby (Rototiller)** ensures infinite resources by recycling key cards

---

## Attacker Choice: Slaking ex

### Why Slaking ex?
- **Massive damage output**
- **340 HP**, allowing it to tank attacks Dragapult ex cannot
- Can **one-shot major threats**:
  - Regidrago VSTAR
  - Lugia VSTAR
  - Tag Team Pokémon

### Additional Advantages
- **Colorless typing**
  - Can be searched by **Fan Rotom**
- Ability drawback is often irrelevant:
  - Many decks rely on Pokémon ex/V
  - Can be disabled via **Path to the Peak**

---

## Energy Package
- **Double Colorless Energy**: Primary attacker fuel
- **Jet Energy + Capture Energy**: Flexible alternatives
- **Triple Acceleration Energy**
  - Useful vs Energy-taxing effects (Wondrous Labyrinth Prism Star, Goomy)
  - Discard drawback is irrelevant due to **Great Swing**

---

## Matchup Tools & Tech Cards

- **Karen**
  - Recovers Bunnelby
  - Strong vs **Regidrago** and **Lugia**
  - Combined with Item lock, prevents **Battle Compressor**
- **Pokémon Ranger**
  - Tech against **Shadow Rider Calyrex**
  - Removes effects like **Shadow Mist**, which otherwise shuts down the deck

---

## Tips & Play Notes

- Prioritize **Turn 1 Guzma & Hala** above everything else
- Always protect **Bunnelby** — it is the backbone of your resource loop
- Use **Path to the Peak** strategically to neutralize problematic Abilities
- Slaking ex should only attack when necessary — control comes first
- Don’t rush knockouts; winning often comes from **opponent resource exhaustion**
- The deck excels in long games — play patiently and methodically

---

## Summary
Vileplume / Slaking ex is a **high-skill control deck** that rewards precise sequencing, resource management, and matchup knowledge.
It combines one of the strongest Item locks in the format with a brutally efficient attacker, creating a game state where many opponents simply cannot play Pokémon.

A true control deck — oppressive, consistent, and extremely punishing when piloted well.
