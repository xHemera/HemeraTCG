# TeraZard

![Decklist](expanded-decks/assets/decklist/Charizard.png)

## Overview
**Charizard ex / Inteleon** is a **midrange evolution deck** that translates surprisingly well into Expanded.
While it isn’t as dominant as Regidrago VSTAR or Lugia VSTAR, it remains a **serious and respected archetype**, combining extreme bulk, scalable damage, and one of the most consistent Trainer-search engines in the format.

The deck plays a **measured, toolbox-oriented game**, leveraging Shady Dealings to answer threats turn by turn rather than overwhelming the opponent with raw speed.

---

## Why Charizard ex Works

### Bulk & Damage Scaling
- **330 HP**
  - Survives:
    - Regidrago VSTAR copying *Dragon Ascent* (even with Muscle Band)
    - Lugia VSTAR’s *Tempest Dive* with four Powerful Energy
- **Damage output**
  - Starts at **180**
    - Cleans up Bench sitters (Dedenne-GX, Crobat V, etc.)
  - Scales up to **330**
    - Capable of OHKOing almost anything late game
- **Dark typing**
  - Excellent vs:
    - Mew VMAX
    - Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX

Charizard ex is both a **wall** and a **finisher**.

---

## Engine Choice: Inteleon Line

Unlike Standard, Expanded Charizard ex pairs with **Inteleon**, not Pidgeot ex.

### Shady Dealings Core
- **Drizzile / Inteleon (Shady Dealings)**
  - Searches Trainer cards directly
  - Completely unaffected by **Path to the Peak**
- Enables extremely flexible sequencing:
  - Level Ball → Drizzile
  - Shady Dealings → Irida
  - Irida → another Drizzile + Rare Candy
  - Second Drizzile → Evolution Incense → Charizard ex

Effectively acts as a **customizable Arven**, but far more powerful.

---

## Trainer Toolbox

Thanks to Shady Dealings, the deck can afford many **situational one-ofs**:

- **Rare Candy**
  - Accelerates Charizard ex
- **Counter Catcher**
  - Easy to enable since evolution decks often lose a Pokémon first
- **Pokémon Ranger**
  - Critical vs Item lock (e.g. Vikavolt V)
- **VS Seeker**
  - Reuse key Supporters
- **Irida**
  - Binds the entire engine together

This makes the deck extremely adaptable to different matchups.

---

## Inteleon Variants

- **Shady Dealings Inteleon**
  - Continued Trainer access in the mid and late game
- **Quick Shooting Inteleon**
  - Places damage counters
  - Combined with **Muscle Band**, enables clean KO math:
    - Example: 240 + 20 + 20 to KO a VSTAR after two Prizes

This flexibility allows Charizard ex to hit exact numbers rather than overcommitting.

---

## Mobility & Healing

With **Scoop Up Net banned**, the deck adapts using:
- **Professor Turo’s Scenario**
- **Scoop Up Cyclone (Ace Spec)**

These options:
- Reuse Drizzile/Inteleon Abilities
- Heal or reset damaged Charizard ex
- Improve longevity in grindy games

---

## Meta Techs & Counters

The deck includes targeted answers to top threats:

- **Temple of Sinnoh**
  - Strong vs Regidrago VSTAR and Lugia VSTAR
- **Lysandre Prism Star**
  - Lost Zones key attackers or Supporters
  - Especially punishing vs Regidrago
- **Klefki**
  - Counter to Wobbuffet
- **Field Blower**
  - Deals with Garbodor Tool lock
- Other answers to Wobbuffet:
  - Silent Lab
  - Escape Rope (situational)

---

## Flex Slots & Variations

Charizard ex has room to adapt:

- **Octillery (Rapid Strike / Bibarel-style)**
  - Searchable via Level Ball and Irida
  - Rapid Strike pre-evolution can be fetched by Sobble
- Additional Stadiums, tech Pokémon, or healing options depending on the metagame

---

## Tips & Play Notes

- Don’t rush Charizard ex — set up the Inteleon engine first
- Plan Prize mapping carefully; damage scales with opponent’s Prizes
- Use Shady Dealings proactively to stay ahead, not reactively
- Counter Catcher timing is often game-defining
- Charizard ex excels in **long, interactive games**

---

## Strengths
- Extremely bulky main attacker
- Precise Trainer-based consistency
- Strong into Mew and Shadow Rider
- Mostly uses recent cards (newcomer-friendly)

## Weaknesses
- Slower than top-tier combo decks
- Relies on Abilities
- Requires careful sequencing
- Less explosive without Scoop Up Net

---

## Summary
**Charizard ex / Inteleon** is a **solid, intelligent midrange deck** that thrives on consistency, bulk, and adaptability.
While not the absolute top of the format, it has reshaped the metagame by pressuring Mew and Shadow Rider and forcing Lugia variants to evolve.

A great choice for players who enjoy **structured turns, toolbox answers, and meaningful decisions** — and one of the best gateways into Expanded play.
