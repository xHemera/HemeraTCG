## Overview
**Regidrago VSTAR** is the **flagship deck of the Expanded format**: flexible, explosive, and brutally efficient when piloted well.
While it exists in Standard, Expanded is where the deck truly shines thanks to **Dragon synergies**, **discard engines**, and **powerful legacy cards**.

My decklist keeps the classic Regidrago core but pushes the concept further with:
- A **broader Dragon attack toolbox**
- More **disruption and lock options**
- Stronger **tempo control** against fast and combo-heavy decks

Rather than being a pure damage deck, this version plays as a **toolbox-control hybrid** that adapts every turn to the matchup.

---

## Core Concept

### Apex Dragon Toolbox
- **Regidrago VSTAR – Apex Dragon**
  - Copies the attacks of Dragon Pokémon in the discard pile
  - Turns the discard into a second hand
- **Double Dragon Energy**
  - Makes Apex Dragon easy to use as early as turn 2
- **Battle Compressor + Mysterious Treasure**
  - Quickly stock the discard with the exact attacks you need

This makes Regidrago one of the most **consistent and flexible attackers** in the format.

---

## Dragon Attack Package (Key Difference)

My list runs a **wide spread of Dragons** to answer different board states:

- **Dialga-GX**
  - *Timeless GX*: Take an extra turn (once per game)
- **Noivern-GX / Noivern ex**
  - Item lock and disruption
- **Dragapult ex**
  - Spread pressure and bench control
- **Salamence ex** (Will be replaced by Mega Dragonite ex when available)
  - High raw damage option
- **Kyurem**
  - Additional damage coverage
- **Latias ex**
  - Mobility and board control

This variety allows Regidrago to:
- OHKO large Pokémon
- Lock Items
- Disrupt tempo
- Apply spread pressure
—all from a single attacker.

## Discard & Resource Engine

Expanded discard synergies are what elevate Regidrago:

- **Battle Compressor**
  - Sends Dragons and Supporters to the discard instantly
- **VS Seeker**
  - Allows the deck to run many **1-of Supporters**
- **Legacy Star**
  - Emergency discard setup when resources are tight

This engine makes the deck resilient even in grindy games.

---

## Disruption & Control Elements

Unlike more linear builds, my list leans into **active disruption**:

- **Wobbuffet**
  - Early-game Ability lock
  - Can be Active turn 1 to slow setup
- **Budew**
  - Early Item disruption
- **Path to the Peak**
  - Shuts down opposing engines
- **Parallel City**
  - Bench control vs wide setups
- **Faba**
  - Permanent removal of Double Dragon Energy
- **Counter Catcher**
  - Punishes aggressive Prize taking
- **Megaton Blower**
  - Cleans up Tools and Stadiums in one swing

These tools make Regidrago far harder to rush down.

## Damage Modifiers & Math

- **Muscle Band (x2)**
  - Crucial for OHKO thresholds
  - Enables plays like:
    - Dragon Ascent + Muscle Band
- Apex Dragon damage math should always be planned **one turn ahead**

## Matchup Dynamics

### Strengths
- Can adapt to almost any deck
- Strong vs setup-dependent engines
- Excellent midgame control
- High skill ceiling

### Weaknesses
- Low Energy count
- Vulnerable to:
  - Energy denial
  - Girafarig-style Lost Zone disruption
- Requires careful Battle Compressor usage

---

## Tips & Play Notes

- Never discard Dragons carelessly — every choice matters
- Assume the opponent has Energy denial
- Wobbuffet timing can decide entire games
- Timeless GX should be planned, not improvised
- Don’t overextend draw Pokémon onto the Bench
- Regidrago rewards **patience and foresight**, not brute force

---

## Ace Spec Considerations
While many Regidrago lists favor:
- **Scoop Up Cyclone** (defensive, healing, anti-trap)
- **Prime Catcher** (aggression and tempo)

My list prioritizes **flexibility and disruption**, relying more on in-game positioning than Ace Spec swings.

## Summary
Regidrago VSTAR is the **perfect showcase of Expanded’s depth**:
a deck that combines raw power, toolbox flexibility, and layered disruption.

My version trades some linear explosiveness for **interaction, resilience, and decision-making**, making it exceptionally strong in skilled hands — but equally punishing if misplayed.

A true **thinking player’s deck**, and one of the best entry points into Expanded Pokémon.
